# Scan credits

Four page scans of printed material published in the United States before 1929, each cropped, converted to grayscale and resized. `truth.json` holds a transcription of each page, made for this example from the full-resolution original. It keeps the spelling and punctuation, and in running text the printed line breaks and end-of-line hyphens. The fare table is transcribed one table cell per line, so a cell printed on two lines is on one line.
